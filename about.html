Meet Nataliia Lauria

Musician, educator, author, and visual artist guiding students through a warm, creative, and deeply personal musical journey.

A Life Shaped by Music and Art

Nataliia Lauria is the founder of NatArt Vibes, a boutique artistic studio where music, creativity, and personal expression come together in a warm and inspiring atmosphere. Her path brings together performance, thoughtful teaching, writing, and visual art.

An Artist, Educator, and Mentor

After studying at Kiev Music College and the Kiev Conservatory, she built a professional path that united performance, teaching, and artistic exploration.

Orchestra Experience

Her path in music has included work with respected institutions such as the Kiev Opera Theatre, the Kiev Opera Theatre for Children and Youth, the Cairo Opera, the Malta Philharmonic Orchestra, and the School of Arts at the University of Malta.

Trio and Quartet Performance

As a performer, she appeared as first violinist in the Lakota Piano Trio and the Yaro String Quartet, taking part in various international festivals.

Teaching and Mentorship

All graduates from her class went on to build solo, teaching, and orchestral careers with the Malta Philharmonic Orchestra and in orchestras across Europe. For the past three years, she has continued this work in Calgary with a thoughtful, personal, and deeply artistic approach to teaching.

About the Studio

NatArt Vibes was created as a place where talent can be gently discovered, nurtured, and brought to the surface through music, creativity, and personal guidance.

Teaching Philosophy

Music education should be thoughtful, inspiring, and deeply personal, helping students build confidence, imagination, discipline, and freedom of expression.

Creative World

Beyond music, Nataliia Lauria writes under the name Flora Noelle Lark and shares a wider artistic vision through literature and painting.
